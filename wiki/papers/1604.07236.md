# Towards Real-Time, Country-Level Location Classification of Worldwide Tweets

## TL;DR
This paper presents a real-time system for geolocating tweets at the country level using inherent features.

## Problem
The problem addressed is the lack of explicit location information in most tweets, making it difficult to infer a user's country of origin. Previous research has focused on limited geographical areas or countries, and methods that can be applied globally are often not feasible in real-time due to the need for extensive feature extraction.

## Method
The authors use eight tweet-inherent features, including tweet content, metadata, and user information, to classify tweets by country of origin. They experiment with different combinations of these features to find the most effective approach.

## Results
The results show that using a combination of tweet content and metadata can lead to substantial improvements in classification accuracy, with improvements of between 20% and 50%. The authors also find that certain features, such as tweet content and user self-reported location, are particularly useful for determining the country of origin.

## Limitations
The difficulty of achieving accurate classification increases slightly for countries with multiple commonalities, especially for English and Spanish speaking countries. Unknown limitations.
