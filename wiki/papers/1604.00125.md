# AttSum: Joint Learning of Focusing and Summarization with Neural Attention

## TL;DR
AttSum is a novel summarization system that jointly learns focusing and summarization with neural attention, achieving competitive performance without hand-crafted features.

## Problem
Current extractive query-focused summarization systems often perform relevance ranking and saliency ranking in isolation, leading to suboptimal results due to inadequate supervision from reference summaries.

## Method
AttSum proposes a joint model that uses a neural attention mechanism to learn distributed representations for sentences and document clusters, automatically learning features without relying on hand-crafted features.

## Results
Extensive experiments on DUC query-focused summarization benchmark datasets show that AttSum outperforms widely-used summarization systems with competitive performance, achieving results comparable to those with rich hand-crafted features.
