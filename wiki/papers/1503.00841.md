# Robustly Leveraging Prior Knowledge in Text Classification

## TL;DR
This paper proposes three regularization terms to make text classification models more robust by leveraging prior knowledge.

## Problem
The problem addressed in this paper is how to leverage prior knowledge to guide the learning process in natural language processing tasks, while avoiding bias and making the model more robust.

## Method
The authors propose three regularization terms on top of Generalized Expectation Criteria (GE): a regularization term associated with neutral features, the maximum entropy of class distribution regularization term, and the KL divergence between reference and predicted class distribution. These terms are designed to make the model more robust by reducing sensitivity to prior knowledge.

## Results
Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approaches outperform baselines and work much more robustly, with remarkable improvements in performance.
