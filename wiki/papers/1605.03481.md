# Tweet2Vec: Character-Based Distributed Representations for Social Media

## TL;DR
Tweet2Vec learns vector-space representations of social media posts by composing them from constituent characters, improving performance over word-level approaches.

## Problem
Traditional NLP approaches fail on social media text due to informal language, spelling errors, and large vocabulary size.

## Method
The proposed model uses a Bi-directional Gated Recurrent Unit (Bi-GRU) neural network to learn tweet representations from character sequences, with the goal of generalizing to out-of-vocabulary words and sequences at test time.

## Results
The model outperforms a word-level baseline in predicting user-annotated hashtags associated with tweets, especially when input contains many out-of-vocabulary words or unusual character sequences.
