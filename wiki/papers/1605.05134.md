# A Semi-automatic Method for Efficient Detection of Stories on Social Media

## TL;DR
A semi-automatic tool for efficiently tracking stories on Twitter during real-world events using natural language processing and clustering algorithms.

## Problem
Twitter's vast volume of tweets about events makes it difficult to identify and track relevant information, with many non-assertive messages cluttering the feed. Assertions are crucial for story detection, but they account for less than half of all event-related tweets.

## Method
The tool combines a boolean query search, an Assertion Detector module using a trained speech-act classifier, and Hierarchical Clustering to identify and group semantically similar assertions in tweets.

## Results
The system increased the speed and accuracy of story tracking compared to conventional methods, with a user study showing improved performance. The assertion detector achieved an F-score of .86 on classifying assertions using 20-fold cross-validation.
