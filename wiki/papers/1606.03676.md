# External Lexical Information for Multilingual Part-of-Speech Tagging

## TL;DR
This paper compares the performance of four part-of-speech tagging systems using different approaches, including feature-based and neural-based methods.

## Problem
The problem addressed in this paper is to compare the impact of external lexicons and word vector representations on the accuracy of part-of-speech models for multiple languages.

## Method
The authors compared four systems: two feature-based (MEMMs and CRFs) and two neural-based (bi-LSTMs). They used datasets covering 16 languages and evaluated the performance of each system using different metrics.

## Results
The results show that all four approaches perform similarly, but better performances are obtained with feature-based models on lexically richer datasets and with neural-based methods on datasets with less lexical variability.
