# A matter of words: NLP for quality evaluation of Wikipedia medical articles

## TL;DR
The paper proposes a natural language processing approach for evaluating the quality of Wikipedia medical articles using domain-specific features.

## Problem
Wikipedia's open editing policy can lead to unreliable information, and machine learning techniques are needed to evaluate article quality, including readability, reliability, completeness, and coherence.

## Method
The authors use machine learning techniques to extract biomedical concepts from text using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and dictionaries-based techniques, focusing on domain-specific features such as vocabulary, codes, and document type.

## Results
The results show that considering domain-oriented features can improve quality evaluation compared to existing solutions, especially for articles with low classification accuracy.
