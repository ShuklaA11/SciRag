# MGNC-CNN: A Simple Approach to Exploiting Multiple Word Embeddings for Sentence Classification

## TL;DR
MGNC-CNN is a simple CNN architecture that combines multiple word embeddings for sentence classification.

## Problem
The problem addressed by this paper is how to combine different pre-trained word embeddings, which may have different dimensions and capture different aspects of language, to improve NLP models, particularly CNNs.

## Method
MGNC-CNN treats different word embeddings as distinct groups and applies CNNs independently to each, generating corresponding feature vectors that are then concatenated at the classification layer. A group regularization strategy is used to penalize weights associated with subcomponents generated from respective embedding sets.

## Results
The proposed MGNC-CNN consistently outperforms baseline models and requires substantially less training time, achieving state-of-the-art performance on multiple sentence classification tasks in an order of magnitude more efficient manner than comparable architectures.
