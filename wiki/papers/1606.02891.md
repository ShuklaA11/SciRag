# Edinburgh Neural Machine Translation Systems for WMT 16

## TL;DR
The authors present neural machine translation systems for four language pairs, achieving improvements in BLEU scores and human evaluation.

## Problem
The paper addresses the problem of improving neural machine translation systems for four language pairs: English↔Czech, English↔German, English↔Romanian, and English↔Russian. The authors aim to improve the performance of these systems by experimenting with various techniques.

## Method
The authors use an attentional encoder-decoder architecture with BPE subword segmentation for open-vocabulary translation. They also experiment with automatic back-translations of monolingual data as additional training data, pervasive dropout, and target-bidirectional models. The implementation is based on the dl4mt-tutorial and uses Adadelta for training.

## Results
The authors achieve improvements in BLEU scores ranging from 4.3 to 11.2 over their baseline systems. In human evaluation, their systems were the best constrained system for 7 out of 8 translation directions.
