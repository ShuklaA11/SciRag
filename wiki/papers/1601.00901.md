# Joint learning of ontology and semantic parser from text

## TL;DR
A novel approach to joint learning of ontology and semantic parser from text is proposed.

## Problem
Traditional methods for ontology learning only capture salient concepts, not sufficient for semantic parsing, and are limited to a particular domain. Existing ontologies cannot represent arbitrary sentences.

## Method
The method uses semi-automatic induction of a context-free grammar from semantically annotated text, which parses the text into semantic trees. The grammar and trees are used to learn the ontology on several levels - classes, instances, taxonomic and non-taxonomic relations. Curriculum learning is applied, starting with simple sentences and proceeding to more complex ones.

## Results
The approach was evaluated on the first sentences of Wikipedia pages describing people, achieving scalable parsing of a large dataset of sentences. Relations between instances were learned using a supervised method.
