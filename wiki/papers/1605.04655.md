# Joint Learning of Sentence Embeddings for Relevance and Entailment

## TL;DR
The paper proposes a neural model for jointly learning sentence embeddings for relevance and entailment in text-based question answering.

## Problem
The problem addressed is Recognizing Textual Entailment within an Information Retrieval context, where multiple pieces of evidence with varying relevance must be evaluated to determine the answer to a binary natural language question.

## Method
The proposed model uses neural networks for sentence embeddings and integrates evidence for entailment. It jointly trains the sentence embeddings to model relevance and entailment without explicit supervision on individual evidence snippets.

## Results
The model improves the state-of-art on the MCTest dataset and achieves good results on a small dataset of school test questions.
