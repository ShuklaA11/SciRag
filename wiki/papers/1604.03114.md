# Conversational Flow in Oxford-style Debates

## TL;DR
A computational framework is proposed to track conversational flow in Oxford-style debates and its effect on debate outcomes.

## Problem
Public debates allow for interactive discussion, but it's unclear how this affects the outcome of a debate.

## Method
The authors introduce a methodology to identify debating strategies by tracking talking points and their usage throughout the debate. They use transcripts from 108 Oxford-style debates to apply this framework and show that conversational flow patterns are predictive of which debater is more likely to persuade an audience.

## Results
Winners tend to make better use of interactive components, actively pursuing opponents' points rather than promoting their own ideas (20% delta vs. 5%).
