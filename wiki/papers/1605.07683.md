## TL;DR
This paper proposes a testbed to evaluate end-to-end dialog systems in goal-oriented applications, specifically restaurant reservation.

## Problem
Traditional dialog systems require domain-specific handcrafting, making it hard to scale up to new domains. End-to-end dialog systems have shown promising performance in chit-chat settings but may not carry over to goal-oriented conversations.

## Method
The paper proposes a set of five tasks within the goal-oriented context of restaurant reservation, testing crucial capabilities such as dialog management, querying Knowledge Bases, and interpreting output from queries. The tasks are designed to favor reproducibility and comparisons, and are lightweight and easy to use.

## Results
An end-to-end dialog system based on Memory Networks reached promising yet imperfect performance, learning to perform non-trivial operations. Similar results were observed on data extracted from an online concierge service.
