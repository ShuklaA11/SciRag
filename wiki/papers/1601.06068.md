# Paraphrase Generation from Latent-Variable PCFGs for Semantic Parsing

## TL;DR
The paper proposes a novel framework for paraphrasing questions using latent-variable PCFGs to improve semantic parsing in open-domain question answering.

## Problem
Current semantic parsing approaches are vulnerable to unseen words and phrases due to their sensitivity to word order and mismatch between natural language and knowledge bases. This makes it difficult to answer questions that require inference, such as "What do people in Czech Republic speak?"

## Method
The proposed framework uses latent-variable PCFGs to generate paraphrases of the input question without requiring aligned parallel corpora or hand-annotated grammars. The L-PCFG is estimated using the spectral method of Narayan and Cohen (2015), which enables sparse grammar estimates and compact models.

## Results
The evaluation experiments on the WebQuestions benchmark dataset show that the performance of the semantic parser improves over strong baselines, with a significant improvement in accuracy when using generated paraphrases.
