# Spatial Concept Acquisition for a Mobile Robot that Integrates Self-Localization and Unsupervised Word Discovery from Spoken Sentences

## TL;DR
A novel unsupervised learning method for robots to learn words related to places from human speech and use them for self-localization.

## Problem
The problem addressed is that robots need to acquire novel concepts and vocabulary from sensor information, including the names of places and their corresponding spatial areas. This requires dealing with uncertainty such as movement errors, sensor noise, and speech recognition errors.

## Method
The proposed method, SpCoA, integrates a generative model for self-localization and unsupervised word segmentation in uttered sentences via latent variables related to spatial concepts. It uses multiple candidates of phonemes from speech recognition results to suppress variability and associates names of places with words and positions.

## Results
Experimental results showed that SpCoA enabled robots to acquire the names of places from speech sentences and effectively utilize them for self-localization, reducing uncertainty in self-positioning.
