# Stereotyping and Bias in the Flickr30K Dataset

## TL;DR
This paper examines biases and stereotypes in the Flickr30K dataset, a collection of image descriptions used to train neural networks.

## Problem
The authors question the assumption that the Flickr30K dataset's descriptions are based solely on the images, and instead find evidence of subjective interpretations and stereotypical descriptions. This can lead to biased language models that propagate harmful stereotypes.

## Method
The paper presents a taxonomy of unwarranted inferences resulting from stereotypes and prejudices, and discusses methods to analyze the data to detect biases. Examples are provided from the Flickr30K dataset, highlighting linguistic bias and speculation about images.

## Results
The study finds that descriptions in the Flickr30K dataset often contain information that cannot be obtained from the image alone, leading to stereotypical descriptions and potential biases in language models. For example, male managers and female subordinates are frequently described.
