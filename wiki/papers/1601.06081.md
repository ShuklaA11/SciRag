# Why Do Urban Legends Go Viral?

## TL;DR
Urban legends are viral stories that balance credibility with incredibility, using news-like details and emotional storytelling.

## Problem
The paper addresses the phenomenon of urban legends spreading rapidly across communities, despite being unverified or false. Urban legends have gained new lifeblood with the advent of the Internet, making it essential to understand their characteristics and appeal.

## Method
The authors use Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools for a quantitative analysis of urban legend characteristics, focusing on the tension between credible and incredible elements. They investigate features such as simplicity, unexpectedness, concreteness, credibility, emotionality, and narrativity.

## Results
The study finds that urban legends mimic news details (who, where, when) to be credible and are emotional and readable like fairy tales to be catchy and memorable. The authors also demonstrate the possibility of recognizing urban legends using machine learning experiments based on these simple features.
