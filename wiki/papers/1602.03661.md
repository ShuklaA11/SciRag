# On the emergence of syntactic structures: quantifying and modelling duality of patterning *

## TL;DR
The paper introduces measures to quantify duality of patterning in language and models its emergence using a multi-agents scheme.

## Problem
Duality of patterning, where languages are organized at two distinct levels, is a core design feature of human language. However, the question remains open on how this structure could have emerged, and there is no clear mathematical framework to quantify it.

## Method
The authors introduce measures to quantify combinatoriality and compositionality in language using datasets from real languages, including dictionaries and a word-association game. They also use a multi-agents modeling scheme, the Blending Game, to model the emergence of duality of patterning.

## Results
The theoretical predictions of the Blending Game are in surprisingly good agreement with empirical data, suggesting that duality of patterning can emerge from pure cultural dynamics.
