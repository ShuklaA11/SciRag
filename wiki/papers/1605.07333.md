# Combining Recurrent and Convolutional Neural Networks for Relation Classification

## TL;DR
This paper investigates neural architectures for relation classification and proposes a new context representation for convolutional neural networks.

## Problem
Relation classification is the task of assigning sentences with two marked entities to a predefined set of relations, which has been improved by applying neural networks in recent research.

## Method
The authors propose extended middle context, a new input representation for relation classification, and connectionist bi-directional recurrent neural networks with ranking loss function. They also combine convolutional and recurrent neural networks using a simple voting scheme.

## Results
The proposed models achieve state-of-the-art results on the SemEval 2010 benchmark dataset, outperforming previous results by applying neural networks.
