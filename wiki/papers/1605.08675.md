# Boosting Question Answering by Deep Entity Recognition

## TL;DR
This paper presents RAFAEL, a Polish question answering system that extracts entities from open-domain plain-text knowledge bases.

## Problem
The problem addressed in this paper is the difficulty of designing question answering systems for languages with complex syntax rules and limited resources, such as Polish. Current solutions are often not easily transferable to other languages and require significant hardware resources.

## Method
The authors introduce a novel technique called Deep Entity Recognition (DeepER), which generalizes traditional Named Entity Recognition by assigning entities to WordNet synsets instead of predefined categories. This approach allows for more accurate answers and is implemented in the RAFAEL system, which uses an open-domain plain-text knowledge base in Polish.

## Results
The results show that DeepER outperforms traditional NER approaches in answering questions that demand answers beyond traditional named entity categories. The system was able to answer over a thousand questions from a quiz TV show with high accuracy.
