# Recurrent Neural Network Encoder with Attention for Community Question Answering

## TL;DR
A recurrent neural network encoder with attention is applied to community question answering tasks, achieving a 10% improvement in MAP score.

## Problem
The paper addresses the problem of automating community question answering forums by ranking related questions and comments for users. The challenges include informal language usage, diverse content, and variation in length of both questions and comments.

## Method
The proposed method uses a recurrent neural network encoder with attention to learn representations automatically without relying on handcrafted features. An LSTM unit is used with an associated attention mechanism to encode pairs of sentences into dense vectors for predicting relationships.

## Results
The experiments show a 10% improvement in MAP score compared to an information retrieval-based approach, and comparable performance to a strong handcrafted feature-based method.
