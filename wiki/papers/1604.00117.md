# Domain Adaptation of Recurrent Neural Networks for Natural Language Understanding

## TL;DR
This paper proposes a multi-task learning approach to improve slot filling models for natural language understanding using recurrent neural networks.

## Problem
The problem addressed in this paper is the scalability of slot filling models for natural language understanding, particularly when dealing with multiple target tasks or domains. Traditional approaches require large amounts of training data, which can be impractical and costly.

## Method
The proposed method uses multi-task learning to share patterns learned from one task to improve performance on other tasks. The approach leverages a bidirectional LSTM architecture and incorporates open vocabulary embeddings to enable generalization to unseen words. Only the output layer is individual to each app, while most model parameters are shared.

## Results
The proposed multi-task model delivers better performance with less data by leveraging patterns learned from other tasks. Results show improved performance on all tasks due to information sharing between apps, with a large cost savings and scalability to large numbers of applications.
