# Character-Level Question Answering with Attention

## TL;DR
A character-level encoder-decoder framework improves question answering with a structured knowledge base by reducing parameters and requiring less data.

## Problem
The paper addresses the challenges of mapping natural language questions to their corresponding KB queries, including paraphrases, unseen entities, and large-scale KBs.

## Method
The proposed model uses a character-level LSTM encoder to embed questions, extracts embeddings for questions, predicates, and entities from character-level representations, and applies a general interaction function between question and KB embeddings to determine output.

## Results
The model achieves 70.9% accuracy on the SimpleQuestions dataset without ensembles, outperforming previous state-of-the-art by 8.2%, and requires significantly less data compared to previous work.
