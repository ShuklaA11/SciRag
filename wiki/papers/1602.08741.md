# Gibberish Semantics: How Good is Russian Twitter in Word Semantic Similarity Task?

## TL;DR
This paper evaluates the use of Russian Twitter data for word semantic similarity task using Word2Vec model.

## Problem
The study addresses the applicability of language models to languages other than English, specifically focusing on Russian. Traditional corpora used for word semantic similarity tasks are not suitable for Russian due to its morphological complexity.

## Method
The authors use a corpus-based approach with Word2Vec model trained on Russian Twitter stream data. They compare the results with single-corpus trained models and combination of multiple corpora.

## Results
Results show that vectors trained on Twitter corpus are comparable in accuracy with other single-corpus trained models, although the best performance is currently achieved by combination of multiple corpora.
