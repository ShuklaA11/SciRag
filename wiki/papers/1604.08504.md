# Detecting "Smart" Spammers On Social Network: A Topic Model Approach

## TL;DR
A novel spammer classification approach based on Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) is proposed to detect 'smart' spammers on social networks.

## Problem
Spammer detection on social networks is challenging due to the emergence of 'smart' spammers who resemble legitimate users and are difficult to identify. These spammers can sway public opinion and cause distrust in the platform.

## Method
The proposed approach uses Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) to extract topic distribution patterns from user tweets, capturing both local and global information. Two new topic-based features, Local Outlier Standard Score (LOSS) and Global Outlier Standard Score (GOSS), are extracted to distinguish human-like spammers from legitimate users.

## Results
The proposed method outperforms other state-of-the-art methods in terms of averaged F1-score on two benchmark datasets. The combination of LOSS and GOSS features effectively distinguishes human-like spammers.
