# Empirical Gaussian priors for cross-lingual transfer learning

## TL;DR
The paper proposes using empirical Gaussian priors for cross-lingual transfer learning in sequence models.

## Problem
Cross-lingual part-of-speech (POS) tagging is challenging due to noisy training data and high Rademacher complexity, making it prone to overfitting. Current methods are limited to a small minority of languages with sufficient labeled training data.

## Method
The authors propose using empirical Gaussian priors estimated from k source language models to regularize sequence model learning algorithms. This approach leads to significantly better performance in multi-source transfer set-ups and reduces Rademacher complexity.

## Results
Using empirical Gaussian priors results in much lower Rademacher complexity, integrated variance, and is superior to optimally weighted model interpolation. Performance improves by using multiple source languages for annotation projection.
