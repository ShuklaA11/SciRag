# A Label Semantics Approach to Linguistic Hedges

## TL;DR
A model for linguistic hedges 'very' and 'quite' is introduced within the label semantics framework.

## Problem
The paper addresses the need for a semantically grounded account of linguistic hedges to increase accuracy in rules or queries while maintaining human interpretability of results.

## Method
The proposed model uses the label semantics framework, which quantifies an agent's subjective uncertainty about the extent of application of a concept. The model combines this approach with conceptual spaces and prototype theory to give a formalisation of concepts as based on a prototype and a threshold, located in a conceptual space.

## Results
The authors provide formulations of the hedges 'very' and 'quite', showing that they can be composed with themselves and with other functions. They investigate the behaviour in the limit of composition.
